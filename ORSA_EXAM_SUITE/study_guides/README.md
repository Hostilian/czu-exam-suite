# ORSA Final Exam Study Suite
This study suite is designed specifically to help you pass the **Operations Research and Systems Analysis (ORSA)** exam (course codes **EAE58E, EAEJ1E**) at PEF ČZU. Since you have already passed the credit requirements, these guides focus **exclusively** on the material tested in the final exam, ensuring you spend your study time only on what is necessary to pass.

---

## 📅 Exam Structure & Rules
* **Total Time**: 60 minutes
* **Total Points**: 100 points
* **Oral Exam**: None (no oral part)
* **Minimum to Pass**: **60 points total**, with the following strict sub-limits:
  * You must obtain **at least 40% (20 / 50 points)** in Part 1.
  * You must obtain **at least 40% (20 / 50 points)** in Part 2.
* **Lecture Bonus Points**: Any extra points earned during the semester will be added to your final score after meeting the pass criteria.

The exam consists of **two parts** administered in Moodle:

### 📝 Part 1: Theory (Max 50 Points)
* **Time Limit**: Strict **30 minutes** (this part closes automatically, and you cannot return to it or save it for later).
* **Format**:
  * **4 Multiple-Choice Questions**: Theoretical questions from the lectures (each question usually has 5 options, with 1 correct answer).
  * **1 Matching Question (8 Items)**: Match 8 terms with their correct definitions or statements.
  * **1 Causal Loop Diagram (CLD) Task**: Analyze or verify a CLD (identify loop polarities, link polarities, delay indicators, feedback loop type).

### 💻 Part 2: Practical Vensim Modeling (Max 50 Points)
* **Time Limit**: The remaining time of the 60 minutes.
* **Format**: You are given a short text description of a system and must construct a **Stock and Flow Diagram (SFD)** in **Vensim PLE**.
* **Submission**: You must upload your solved `.mdl` file directly to the Moodle upload link before the exam time limit expires.
* **Deductions**:
  * Wrong polarity: **-5 points**
  * Wrong feedback loop (missing or non-existing): **-6 points**
  * Wrong type of feedback (e.g., Reinforcing instead of Balancing): **-4 points**
  * Mistake in model structure: **up to -7 points**
  * Wrong connection to a stock variable (connecting stock to stock directly, etc.): **-10 points**
