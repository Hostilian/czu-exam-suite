Detailed Math Rule:

Mathematically, a loop is a product of partial derivatives: $\text{sign}(\text{loop}) = \prod \text{sign}(\text{link}_i)$. A positive times a negative is negative. Thus, any odd count of negative links yields a negative loop product (Balancing), while an even count yields a positive loop product (Reinforcing).

Model Guidelines
Moodle Target Model: Represents viral transmission inside a population of 50,000.
Susceptible population = INTEG(recovery without imunity - infection rate, 49990)
Infected population = INTEG(infection rate - recovery without imunity - recovery with imunity - death rate, 10)
Imune population = INTEG(recovery with imunity, 0)
Total deaths = INTEG(death rate, 0)
infection rate = totla number of risk contacts * share of infected on the total population * INFECTIVITY
totla number of risk contacts = Susceptible population * AVERAGE NUMBER OF RISK CONTACTS
share of infected on the total population = Infected population / (Infected population + Susceptible population + Imune population)
exit rate = Infected population / AVERAGE DUTATION OF INFECTION
recovery rate = exit rate * (1 - MORTALITY)
recovery with imunity = recovery rate * SHARE OF IMUNE
recovery without imunity = recovery rate * (1 - SHARE OF IMUNE)
death rate = exit rate * MORTALITY
Constants: AVERAGE NUMBER OF RISK CONTACTS = 10 | AVERAGE DUTATION OF INFECTION = 7 | INFECTIVITY = 0.05 | SHARE OF IMUNE = 0.5 | MORTALITY = 0.03
Moodle Target Model: Shows demographic feedbacks with GDP per Capita lookup tables.
Population = INTEG(births - deaths, 458)
GDP = INTEG(GDP growth, 430)
births = Population * birth rate
deaths = Population / average life expectancy
GDP growth = GDP * GDP growth rate
GDP per capita = GDP / Population
birth rate = WITH LOOKUP (GDP per capita, ([(0,0.02)-(20,0.05)],(0.44,0.0288),(1.22,0.032),(1.90,0.0307),(20,0.0307)))
GDP growth rate = WITH LOOKUP (Time, ([(0,0)-(2021,0.07)],(1500,0.0007),(1820,0.006),(1950,0.025),(1980,0.06),(1990,0.02),(2021,0.05)))
average life expectancy = WITH LOOKUP (Time, ([(0,0)-(2021,70)],(0,35),(1800,35),(1950,50),(2000,70),(2021,70)))
Initial Time = 1500 | Final Time = 2020 | Time Step = 0.125
Moodle Target Model: Inventory scheduling control with workforce delay and schedule pressure.
Inventory = INTEG(production - shipment, 4000)
Workforce = INTEG(hire rate - quit rate, 50)
shipment = customer orders
customer orders = 1000 * (1 + STEP(0.1, 10))
average order rate = SMOOTH(customer orders, time period for averaging orders)
desired inventory = average order rate * time period for inventory holdings
inventory correction = (desired inventory - Inventory) / time period for reconciling inventory
desired production = average order rate + inventory correction
production = production rate * effect of schedule pressure
production rate = Workforce * average productivity
desired workforce = desired production / average productivity
hire rate = quit rate + workforce correction
quit rate = Workforce / time period of average employment
workforce correction = (desired workforce - Workforce) / time period to hire new workers
schedule pressure = desired production / production rate
effect of schedule pressure = WITH LOOKUP(schedule pressure, ([(0.8,0.8)-(1.2,1.3)],(0.8,0.875),(0.9,0.875),(1,1),(1.1,1.25),(1.2,1.25)))
