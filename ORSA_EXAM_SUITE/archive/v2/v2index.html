Soft Systems Methodology (SSM) & Archetypes Review

Checkland's 7 Stages of SSM

  1. Stage 1: The Problem Situation (Unstructured): Explore the problem area informally, gathering data.
  2. Stage 2: Problem Situation Expressed (Rich Picture): Cartoon-like diagram representing structures, processes, climate, people, issues, and conflicts.
  3. Stage 3: Root Definitions (CATWOE): Purposeful perspectives holons. Definition format: "A system to do X, by Y, in order to do Z."
  4. Stage 4: Conceptual Model: Logical activity verbs diagram ($7 \pm 2$ activities) with dependencies.
  5. Stage 5: Comparison: Matrix comparing conceptual activities to real-world behaviors.
  6. Stage 6: Desirable & Feasible Changes: Identify culturally/politically feasible interventions.
  7. Stage 7: Action to Improve: Implement modifications, cycling back.

CATWOE Elements

  • C – Customers (Beneficiaries/Victims): Who benefits or suffers from the system's transformations.
  • A – Actors: Stakeholders carrying out the transformation processes.
  • T – Transformation: Core process converting Inputs to Outputs (I ➔ O).
  • W – Weltanschauung (Worldview): The belief system rendering the transformation meaningful.
  • O – Owners: Stakeholders with the authority to start, change, or terminate the system.
  • E – Environmental Constraints: Immutable rules/limitations the system must respect.
