Credit specification

To obtain credit you need

·        Attendance (one unexcused absence)

·        Active participation on tutorials and lectures

·       10 self-tests– The self-test will be available from March 18th until April 26th. You can find them in each topic  in the section „Self-test“ in Moodle. You have to reach at least 60% of the points from each test. Tests are based on theory and calculations as well. Topic for each self-test will be specify in description for each self-test. For each self- test you have 3 attempts. The highest level of obtained points is assigned to your result.

In case, you have more then 1 unexused absence or  lower level (less then 60%) of required points from each self-test, you have to do a reset credit test (available in Moodle).
