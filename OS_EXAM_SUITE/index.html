🌐 IoT & Computing Models
MQTT: Publish/Subscribe model, runs over **TCP** (reliable). Requires a central Broker. Ideal for low-bandwidth telemetry.
CoAP: Request/Response model, RESTful (GET/POST), runs over **UDP** (low overhead). Best for lightweight constrained nodes.
Supercomputer (Batch): Executing massive scientific jobs in parallel (often using large Linux clusters) without manual human intervention.
