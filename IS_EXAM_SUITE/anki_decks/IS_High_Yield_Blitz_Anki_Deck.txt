<div class="anki-card"><div class="anki-category">Data Warehouse</div><div class="anki-q-title">What are the 4 DW characteristics (SINV)?</div></div>	<div class="anki-card-back"><div class="anki-ans"><b>S — Subject-Oriented:</b> Organized around subjects (sales, products)<br><b>I — Integrated:</b> Combines data from multiple sources consistently<br><b>N — Non-Volatile:</b> Data is stable/historical, NOT updated like OLTP<br><b>V — Time-Variant:</b> Every record has timestamp; tracks historical changes</div></div>	IS_Exam_Blitz_data_warehouse

Once loaded, DW data is permanent. This is different from TPS/OLTP where data changes constantly.</div></div>	IS_Exam_Blitz_data_warehouse
<div class="anki-card"><div class="anki-category">Data Warehouse</div><div class="anki-q-title">What is Time-Variant in a Data Warehouse?</div></div>	<div class="anki-card-back"><div class="anki-ans">DW data is <b>connected with time</b> — every record has a timestamp or time period. The DW tracks HISTORICAL changes over time (e.g., sales trends over 5 years), unlike OLTP which only stores current state.</div></div>	IS_Exam_Blitz_data_warehouse

Examples: RSA, used in TLS/HTTPS</div></div>	IS_Exam_Blitz_security

Medium speed both ways.</div></div>	IS_Exam_Blitz_security
