Click each step to expand details about the Procure-to-Pay process in SAP.

01
Purchase Requisition (PR) ME51N
Internal request to purchase goods/services. Created by the department needing the material. Triggers the purchasing process. Can be created automatically by MRP in PP module. Contains: material, quantity, required date, cost center.
↓
02
Request for Quotation / Vendor Selection ME41
Purchasing sends RFQ to potential vendors. Vendor quotations are entered and compared. Best vendor selected based on price, delivery, quality. Vendor master must exist (FK01/XK01).
↓
03
Purchase Order (PO) ME21N
Legal document sent to vendor committing to purchase. Contains: vendor, material, quantity, price, delivery date, plant. PO references PR. Purchasing organization and purchasing group must be configured.
↓
04
Goods Receipt (GR) MIGO
Physical receipt of goods into the warehouse. References the PO. Updates inventory (stock increases). Creates accounting document (debit: stock/GR/IR account). Material document and accounting document created simultaneously.
↓
05
Invoice Verification MIRO
Vendor invoice is entered and matched against PO and GR (3-way match). If quantities and prices match, invoice is posted. Creates accounting document. Discrepancies trigger tolerance checks. AP liability is created.
↓
06
Payment Run F110
Automatic payment program in SAP FI. Selects open invoices due for payment. Considers payment terms, discount deadlines, bank details. Generates payment file for bank. Clears open items in vendor account. Sends remittance advice.

Comparison of cloud service models — a common exam topic.

Model You Manage Provider Manages Examples Responsibility
IaaS OS, runtime, middleware, apps, data Virtualization, servers, storage, networking AWS EC2, Azure VMs, GCP Compute Most Control
PaaS Applications, data OS, runtime, middleware, infrastructure Heroku, Google App Engine, Azure App Service Balanced
SaaS Only configuration & data input Everything — app, OS, infrastructure Salesforce, Office 365, SAP S/4HANA Cloud Least Effort
On-Premises Everything — hardware to application Nothing Self-hosted SAP R/3, legacy systems Full Burden
SAP Cloud Variants
SAP S/4HANA Cloud (SaaS) · SAP BTP (PaaS) · SAP Private Cloud Edition (IaaS/managed)
Key Differentiator
Pizza analogy: Made at home (on-prem), delivered (IaaS/PaaS) or dine-in (SaaS) — who does the work?
Exam Tip
Know who is responsible for what layer in each model. CAPEX (on-prem) vs OPEX (cloud) trade-offs.
